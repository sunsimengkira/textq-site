How it works

From list to ready-to-send in three steps.

  1. 1

    Paste your list.

    Copy rows from your spreadsheet, or import a CSV or Excel file.

  2. 2

    Write once. Personalize each text.

    Use any imported column—like first name, address, or date. Add one image, such as a business card, map, or flyer.

  3. 3

    Review and choose how to send.

    Check recipients and personalized messages. Remove anyone you don’t want to include, then choose how to send.

Sending

Review your messages. Choose how to send.

Send One by One

TextQ opens Apple Messages with the recipient and personalized message already filled in. Review it, then tap Send.

Fast Send

Review the batch in TextQ, then explicitly choose Open Shortcuts & Start Sending for the remaining messages. Requires the TextQ Fast Send Shortcut and sends through Apple Messages.
