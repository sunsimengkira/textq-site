Open-house visitors. Event attendees. Client follow-up. Community outreach.

How it works

From list to ready-to-send in three steps.

  1. 1

    Paste your list.

    Copy rows from your spreadsheet, or import a CSV or Excel file.

  2. 2

    Write once. Personalize each text.

    Use fields such as first name, address, date, or any other column you imported. Add one image too—like a business card, map, or event flyer.

  3. 3

    Review and choose how to send.

    Check the recipients and personalized messages, remove anyone you do not want to include, then choose One-by-One or Fast Send.

Sending

Send from your own number.

Messages are sent through Apple Messages, so replies stay where you already text.

One-by-One

Send One by One

TextQ opens Apple Messages with the recipient and personalized message already filled in. Review it, then tap Send.

Fast Send

Review a few. Fast-send the rest.

After reviewing the batch, you can explicitly start Fast Send for the remaining messages.

Privacy
